How anonymity works

Student posts are shown without names and are associated with the selected house. Feedback is stored in this browser on this device, so someone with access to this device or its browser data may be able to view it.

This is not a secure, centrally managed anonymous collection system, and submissions are not shared across devices. Avoid including names, contact details, or other identifying information in feedback.

Before collecting real student feedback, get approval from your school and advisor and use a secure, school-approved shared platform.
